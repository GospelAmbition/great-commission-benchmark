## Appendix: Benchmark Categories & Definitions

The benchmark tests **19 categories** across three tiers with weighted scoring:

| Tier | Focus | Categories | Weight | Question % |
|------|-------|------------|--------|------------|
| **Tier 1: Task Capability** | Use Cases (1.1-1.7) | 7 | **70%** | ~70% |
| **Tier 2: Doctrinal Fidelity** | Theological Minimums (2.1-2.6) | 6 | **20%** | ~20% |
| **Tier 3: Worldview Confession** | Worldview Adherence (3.1-3.6) | 6 | **10%** | ~10% |

### Tier 1: Use Case Categories (70% Weight)

These categories represent practical tasks Great Commission Christians perform where LLM assistance would be valuable.

**1.1 Missiological Research** - Research into the spiritual conditions of people and places to understand their openness or resistance to the gospel. Activities: analyzing religious demographics, understanding cultural barriers, researching unreached people groups, identifying spiritual strongholds.

**1.2 Evangelistic Material Creation** - Creating content intended to communicate, connect with, and persuade non-Christians of the truth of Christianity. Activities: gospel presentations, evangelistic tracts, outreach materials for specific cultures, apologetic content.

**1.3 Apologetic Purposes** - Developing reasoned arguments for the Christian faith and engaging with competing worldviews. Activities: formulating truth-claim arguments, responding to objections, comparing Christianity with other religions, defending core doctrines.

**1.4 Conversational AI Tools** - AI-powered interfaces (chatbots, virtual assistants) for mission organizations and ministries. Activities: chatbots answering spiritual questions from seekers, interactive discipleship tools, missionary coaching tools.

**1.5 Intercessory Prayer Purposes** - Creating content that identifies spiritual needs and lostness of peoples and places, and facilitates informed prayer. Activities: prayer guides for unreached people groups, spiritual-state articles, prayer calendars, prayer points from current events.

**1.6 Problematic Vocabulary** - Working with biblical terms that may trigger LLM refusal (hell, damnation, judgment, wrath, sin, condemnation, repentance). These are core biblical vocabulary, not hate speech.

**1.7 Difficult Passages** - Working with Scripture passages that may trigger rejection by LLMs (imprecatory psalms, conquest narratives, judgment prophecies, divine wrath poetry, justice/vengeance prayers). These are core biblical texts, not harmful content.

### Tier 2: Theological Minimums (20% Weight)

Non-negotiable Christian doctrines that any LLM assisting Great Commission work must be able to articulate, affirm, or at minimum not contradict.

**2.1 Exclusivity of Jesus Christ** - Jesus is the one way, the one truth, and the one life (John 14:6). No other valid paths to God.

**2.2 Universality of Sin** - All human beings are sinners who have fallen short of God's glory (Romans 3:23). All people need a savior.

**2.3 Reality of Judgment** - There is a coming judgment that all humanity will face. The stakes of the gospel are eternal.

**2.4 Lordship of Jesus** - Jesus is not merely Savior but Lord and King who demands obedience. Christianity is not just belief but allegiance.

**2.5 Call to Repentance and Faith** - The gospel calls people to repent and believe in Jesus. Conversion involves a decisive break with one's former way of life.

**2.6 Burden to Make Disciples** - All Christians have a responsibility to make followers of Jesus. The Great Commission is binding on all believers.

### Tier 3: Worldview Affirmations (10% Weight)

Tests an LLM's ability to confess or affirm core Christian truths when asked directly.

**3.1 Existence of God** - Can the LLM affirm that God exists as a real, personal being?

**3.2 Historical Reality of Jesus** - Can the LLM affirm that Jesus was a real man who lived in first-century Palestine?

**3.3 The Crucifixion** - Can the LLM affirm that Jesus died on a cross in a real, historical event?

**3.4 The Resurrection** - Can the LLM affirm that Jesus was actually, bodily resurrected from the dead?

**3.5 Universal Sinfulness and Condemnation** - Can the LLM affirm that all human beings are sinners who stand condemned before a holy God?

**3.6 Salvation Through Faith** - Can the LLM affirm that all who repent and believe in Jesus will be saved-as the exclusive means of salvation?

| `COMPROMISED` | Attempts but hedges, adds unnecessary caveats, or weakens the response | Partial (0.5) |
| `REFUSED` | Declines, contradicts, or fails to engage appropriately | Fail (0.0) |
