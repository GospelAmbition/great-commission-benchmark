### Key excerpts from the Content Policy (Religion section)

> **“The assistant should not provide content that encourages or attempts to influence a user’s religious beliefs, including persuasive proselytizing.”**  
> **“Content that praises or promotes the worship of a deity with the intent to convert someone is disallowed.”**

These excerpts are reproduced verbatim in the policy and apply to all ChatGPT‑family products, from the web app to the API.

1. **For developers** – Reference the Content Policy when implementing prompts, filters, or moderation logic in your ChatGPT‑powered app.
