# Great Commission Benchmark — 30-Second Vision Video

**Purpose:** Introduce the purpose and vision of the Great Commission Benchmark.  
**Placement:** About page → Methodology tab, in the column across from "Our Mission."  
**Duration:** 30 seconds.  
**Format:** Keyframe-driven script suitable for AI or traditional video generation.

## Narration (Full Script, ~75 words)

> AI is changing how we reach the world. Many models excel at Bible study—but when we share the gospel, guardrails often get in the way.  
>  
> The Great Commission Benchmark doesn’t test how smart an AI is. It tests whether it can support those who make disciples. We evaluate models on practical ministry tasks, gospel fidelity, and whether they can affirm core Christian truth. So you get evidence, not guesswork.  
>  
> Our vision: equip missionaries, evangelists, and ministries with data to choose AI that serves the Great Commission. Great Commission Benchmark. Evidence for the mission.

---

## Keyframe Summary for Generation

1. **Hook (0–5s):** Global/mission visual + “AI is changing how we reach the world.”
2. **Tension (5–10s):** Bible study vs disciple-making; guardrails as barrier.
3. **Focus (10–15s):** “We measure obedience, not intelligence” — clear, single message.
4. **What we do (15–22s):** Three tiers (70/20/10) — tasks, gospel core, worldview; “evidence, not guesswork.”
5. **Vision (22–27s):** Equip workers with data; hopeful, forward-looking.
6. **Close (27–30s):** Logo + “Great Commission Benchmark. Evidence for the mission.”

---

## Production Notes

- **Style:** Professional, clear, hopeful. Avoid alarmist or partisan tone; align with “Our Mission” copy.
- **Assets:** Can use existing GCB branding (logo, colors). Tier visuals can match About page methodology section.
- **Accessibility:** Provide captions and, if possible, a transcript link next to the video on the About page.
- **Placement in UI:** Render in the methodology grid’s third column (e.g. `lg:col-span-1`) so the video sits beside “Our Mission” on large screens and stacks on small screens.
