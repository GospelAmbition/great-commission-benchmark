# Financial Stewardship Agreement

**Last Updated:** December 16, 2025

**Effective Date:** [Date to be set]

---

## 1. Purpose

This document establishes the financial stewardship arrangement for the Great Commission Benchmark project, including the selection of a stewarding ministry and the billing relationship for infrastructure costs.

---

## 2. Stewarding Ministry Selection

### 2.1 Decision

The **Digital Disciple Makers Network** has been selected as the stewarding ministry for finances for the Great Commission Benchmark project.

### 2.2 Candidates Considered

The following ministries were evaluated as candidates for financial stewardship:

1. **Visual Story Network** — Media-focused ministry with experience in digital storytelling
2. **Digital Disciple Makers Network** — Network focused on digital discipleship tools and collaboration
3. **Gospel Ambition** — Ministry with existing technical infrastructure and operational capacity

### 2.3 Rationale

The Digital Disciple Makers Network was selected as the stewarding ministry based on alignment with the Great Commission Benchmark's mission to serve Great Commission Christians and organizations in their digital discipleship and evangelism efforts.

---

## 3. Infrastructure Billing Arrangement

### 3.1 Decision

**Gospel Ambition** will provide the technical infrastructure for the Great Commission Benchmark platform. Gospel Ambition will bill the **Digital Disciple Makers Network** for infrastructure costs incurred in operating the benchmark.

### 3.2 Billing Relationship

| Entity | Role |
|--------|------|
| Gospel Ambition | Infrastructure provider; issues invoices for infrastructure costs |
| Digital Disciple Makers Network | Stewarding ministry; receives and pays infrastructure invoices |

### 3.3 Infrastructure Costs Covered

Infrastructure costs include, but are not limited to:

- Platform hosting (Railway or equivalent)
- Database hosting and backups
- Domain registration and SSL certificates
- Third-party service fees (authentication, analytics, etc.)
- Email delivery services

### 3.4 Billing Frequency

Infrastructure costs will be billed on a [monthly/quarterly] basis, with itemized breakdown of all expenses.

---

## 4. Revenue Flow

### 4.1 Income Sources

The Great Commission Benchmark project may receive revenue from:

- Benchmark hosting contributions ($20 per test)
- Optional round-up donations from users
- Sponsorships from Christian organizations

### 4.2 Fund Management

All revenue will be received and managed by the Digital Disciple Makers Network as the stewarding ministry. These funds will be used to:

1. Pay infrastructure costs invoiced by Gospel Ambition
2. Cover operational expenses
3. Support ongoing development and maintenance
4. Build reserves for sustainability

---

## 5. Accountability

### 5.1 Financial Reporting

Gospel Ambition will provide detailed, itemized invoices for all infrastructure costs, enabling transparency and accountability.

### 5.2 Audit Rights

The Digital Disciple Makers Network reserves the right to audit infrastructure costs to ensure they are reasonable and directly related to the operation of the Great Commission Benchmark.

### 5.3 Review Schedule

This financial stewardship arrangement will be reviewed annually by the founding committee to ensure it continues to serve the project's mission effectively.

---

## 6. Amendments

Any changes to this financial stewardship arrangement must be approved by the founding committee and documented in an updated version of this agreement.
