## 2. Information We Collect

We collect information that you voluntarily provide when you:

### 2.2 Information Automatically Collected

When you use the Service, we may automatically collect:

- **Usage data:** Information about how you interact with the Service, including pages visited, features used, and time spent
- **Device information:** Browser type, operating system, device identifiers, and IP address
- **Log data:** Server logs, error reports, and diagnostic information
- **Cookies and tracking technologies:** See Section 6 for details

### 2.3 Test Results and Model Responses

When you run benchmark tests, we collect:

- **Test results:** Scores, responses, and performance metrics
- **Model responses:** Full responses generated by AI models during testing
- **Test metadata:** Timestamps, model versions, test configurations, and related information

This data is essential for:
- Publishing results to public leaderboards
- Verifying test accuracy and integrity
- Enabling research and analysis
- Maintaining historical records

## 4. How We Share Your Information

### 4.1 Public Sharing

We publish the following information publicly on our leaderboards:

- **Aggregate test results:** Scores, performance metrics, and rankings
- **Model information:** Model names, versions, and configurations used
- **Test metadata:** Timestamps and general test parameters
- **Contributor credits:** Your name (if you choose to be credited)

**Note:** Once results are published to the leaderboard, they become part of the public record and cannot be deleted, even if you request account deletion. This ensures the integrity and historical accuracy of benchmark data.

### 4.2 Service Providers

We share information with third-party service providers who perform services on our behalf:

- **Stripe:** Payment processing (see [Stripe's Privacy Policy](https://stripe.com/privacy))
- **OpenRouter or model providers:** To execute benchmark tests against AI models
- **Hosting and infrastructure providers:** To operate and maintain the Service
- **Analytics providers:** To understand usage patterns and improve the Service

These service providers are contractually obligated to protect your information and use it only for the purposes we specify.

### 4.3 Researchers

We may share anonymized or aggregated data with researchers upon request for legitimate research purposes. Personal identifying information will be removed before sharing.

### 4.4 Model Providers

We may share test results with the providers of the AI models being tested. This includes:
- Their own model's results for transparency and verification
- Aggregate comparisons (without identifying individual testers)

### 4.5 Moderators

We share full test data with authorized moderators who verify test accuracy and integrity. Moderators are bound by confidentiality agreements.

### 4.6 Legal Requirements

We may disclose your information if required by law or in response to:
- Valid legal process (subpoenas, court orders, warrants)
- Government requests or investigations
- Protection of rights, property, or safety
- Enforcement of our Terms of Service or other agreements

### 4.7 Business Transfers

In the event of a merger, acquisition, or sale of assets, your information may be transferred to the acquiring entity, subject to the same privacy protections.
