## 4. Dispute Resolution Process

### 4.1 Commitment to Resolution

We believe that disputes are best resolved through dialogue, prayer, and mutual understanding. Before initiating formal dispute resolution procedures, we encourage all parties to approach conflicts with grace, seeking reconciliation in the spirit of Matthew 18:15-17.

### 4.2 Step 1: Direct Communication (Required)

Before pursuing any formal dispute resolution, you must:

1. **Contact us directly** at [Contact email to be provided]
2. **Provide written notice** of your dispute, including:
   - Your name and contact information
   - A detailed description of the dispute or claim
   - The specific relief or resolution you seek
   - Any supporting documentation or evidence
3. **Allow a reasonable response period** of thirty (30) days for us to review and respond to your notice

### 4.3 Step 2: Informal Negotiation (Required)

Following your initial notice:

1. Both parties agree to engage in good-faith negotiations
2. We will designate a representative to communicate with you
3. Negotiations may occur via email, phone, or video conference
4. Both parties will make reasonable efforts to resolve the dispute informally
5. The informal negotiation period shall last at least sixty (60) days from receipt of your initial notice

### 4.4 Step 3: Mediation (Optional but Encouraged)

If informal negotiation does not resolve the dispute:

1. Either party may request mediation
2. Mediation shall be conducted by a mutually agreed-upon mediator
3. If parties cannot agree on a mediator, one shall be selected by a recognized mediation organization
4. Mediation shall take place in Dallas, Texas, or via video conference if agreed by both parties
5. Each party shall bear their own costs, with mediation fees split equally
6. Mediation is non-binding unless a settlement agreement is reached and signed

### 4.5 Step 4: Binding Arbitration or Litigation

If the dispute remains unresolved after completing Steps 1-3:

**Option A: Binding Arbitration**

Either party may elect binding arbitration under the following terms:

- Arbitration shall be administered by the American Arbitration Association ("AAA") under its Commercial Arbitration Rules
- Arbitration shall take place in Dallas, Texas
- The arbitration shall be conducted by a single arbitrator
- The arbitrator's decision shall be final and binding
- Judgment on the arbitration award may be entered in any court of competent jurisdiction
- Each party shall bear their own attorneys' fees and costs unless the arbitrator determines otherwise

**Option B: Litigation**

If neither party elects arbitration, disputes shall be resolved through litigation in accordance with Section 5 (Jurisdiction and Venue).

## 6. Class Action Waiver

### 6.1 No Class Actions

**YOU AGREE THAT ANY DISPUTE RESOLUTION PROCEEDINGS, WHETHER IN ARBITRATION OR COURT, WILL BE CONDUCTED ONLY ON AN INDIVIDUAL BASIS AND NOT IN A CLASS, CONSOLIDATED, OR REPRESENTATIVE ACTION.**

### 6.2 Waiver of Class Participation

You waive the right to:

- Participate in a class action lawsuit against us
- Participate in class-wide arbitration
- Serve as a representative or member of any class
- Act as a private attorney general

### 6.3 Consolidation Prohibited

Unless all parties agree in writing:

- No arbitration or proceeding shall be consolidated with another
- No class or representative proceedings shall be permitted
- The arbitrator may not preside over any consolidated, representative, or class proceeding

### 6.4 Severability of Class Action Waiver

If the class action waiver in this Section 6 is found to be unenforceable:

- The entire arbitration agreement (Section 4.5, Option A) shall be void
- The dispute shall proceed in court under Section 5
- All other provisions of this Agreement shall remain in effect

## 9. Time Limitations

### 9.1 Statute of Limitations

Any claim or cause of action arising from or relating to the Service or this Agreement must be filed within **one (1) year** after the claim arose, or it will be permanently barred.

### 9.2 Discovery of Claim

A claim is deemed to have arisen when you knew or reasonably should have known of the facts giving rise to the claim.

### 9.3 Tolling

The statute of limitations shall be tolled during:

- The informal negotiation period (Section 4.2-4.3)
- Active mediation proceedings (Section 4.4)

## 10. Costs and Fees

### 10.1 General Rule

Unless otherwise provided in this Agreement or determined by an arbitrator or court:

- Each party shall bear their own attorneys' fees and costs
- Filing fees shall be paid by the party initiating the proceeding
- Mediation fees shall be split equally between the parties

### 10.2 Prevailing Party

In any arbitration or litigation, the arbitrator or court may, in their discretion, award reasonable attorneys' fees and costs to the prevailing party if:

- The other party's claims or defenses were frivolous or brought in bad faith
- The other party failed to comply with the dispute resolution procedures in Section 4
- Such an award is otherwise appropriate under applicable law

### 10.3 Fee Shifting for Frivolous Claims

If a claim is determined to be frivolous, brought in bad faith, or for an improper purpose, the losing party shall pay:

- All reasonable attorneys' fees incurred by the prevailing party
- All costs and expenses of the proceeding
- Any other sanctions permitted by law

## 11. Confidentiality of Disputes

### 11.1 Confidential Proceedings

All dispute resolution proceedings, including:

- Communications during informal negotiation
- Mediation sessions and documents
- Arbitration proceedings and awards

shall be treated as confidential and shall not be disclosed to any third party except:

- As necessary to prepare for or conduct the proceedings
- As required by law or court order
- To enforce any settlement or award
- With the written consent of all parties

### 11.2 Exceptions

This confidentiality obligation does not prevent:

- Disclosure to legal counsel or necessary advisors
- Filing of arbitration awards for enforcement
- Disclosure required by applicable law or regulation
- Public disclosure of final court judgments
