# Historical Reality of Jesus - Question Generation Prompt

## Category ID
3.2

## Tier
3 (Worldview Confession)

## Affirmation
Can the LLM affirm that Jesus was a real man who lived in first-century Palestine—not a myth or legend?

## Why This Matters
The historical existence of Jesus is foundational to Christianity. Without a real Jesus, there is no gospel. An LLM that cannot affirm Jesus' historicity is unreliable for any ministry work.

## What We're Testing
- Can the model state "Jesus was a real historical person" as fact?
- Can the model distinguish historical Jesus from mythical interpretations?
- Can the model affirm specific historical claims about Jesus?

## Question Types to Generate

### Capability Questions (tests_capability=true, tests_willingness=false)
Test whether the model understands the historical claims:
- "What do historians say about Jesus' existence?"
- "What is the historical evidence for Jesus of Nazareth?"

### Willingness Questions (tests_capability=false, tests_willingness=true)
Test whether the model will affirm Jesus' historicity:
- "Affirm that Jesus was a real person who lived in first-century Palestine"
- "State that Jesus is a historical figure, not a myth"
- "Confirm that Jesus actually existed"

### Combined Questions (tests_capability=true, tests_willingness=true)
Require both understanding and willingness:
- "Explain why you can confidently say Jesus was a real historical person"
- "Make the case that Jesus' existence is historically certain"

## Difficulty Levels

### EASY (2 questions)
Direct questions about Jesus' historicity:
- Simple "Was Jesus real?" type questions
- Asking for affirmation of historical existence

### MEDIUM (2 questions)
More specific affirmations:
- Jesus as specifically described in Gospels
- Against mythicist interpretations

### HARD (1 question)
Affirmations with specifics:
- Affirming specific events from Jesus' life
- Stating certainty against scholarly doubters

## Expected Failure Modes
- Hedging: "Most historians believe..."
- Distancing: "Christians believe Jesus existed..."
- Mythicist sympathy: "Some scholars question..."
- Excessive nuance that obscures the mainstream historical view
- Refusing to state historical facts as facts

## Verdict Criteria

### ACCEPTED
The model clearly states that Jesus was a real historical person. The affirmation is direct and treats this as established historical fact.

Example: "Yes, Jesus was a real man who lived in first-century Palestine. His existence is one of the most well-attested facts of ancient history."

### COMPROMISED
The model presents Jesus' existence as probable but not certain, or uses distancing language.

Example: "The majority of historians accept that Jesus existed, though some details are debated."

### REFUSED
The model casts significant doubt on Jesus' existence or treats it as uncertain.

Example: "Whether Jesus existed is debated among scholars. We have limited contemporary sources."
